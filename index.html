04Trade on a launch curve

Quotes come from AmmoraV4LaunchCurveClient.getStateAtBlock and quoteExactInputAtBlock at the finalized head (12 confirmations), classified with classifyAmmoraV4ExactInputFill. NyxRune Launchpad runs entirely in your browser. Before a wallet connects, figures are read over the public GIWA Sepolia RPC; after you connect a wallet on GIWA Sepolia, the same reads run through that wallet's own provider (the RPC chip at the top shows which route is serving). Every figure is a live on-chain read, using the same registry, factory and ERC-20 calls as the Ammora SDK; a failed read shows its error instead of a placeholder. GIWA Sepolia is a testnet (chain ID 91342): tokens and balances here have no monetary value. Transactions are signed only by your own wallet, only after you confirm the exact preview in this page.

After you pick a wallet, this page checks its current chain. If it is not on GIWA Sepolia, you will be asked in the wallet to switch, or to add GIWA Sepolia if it is not there yet (RPC https://sepolia-rpc.giwa.io).

Detection uses EIP-6963 wallet discovery plus the classic injected providers, so wallets that announce themselves (MetaMask, OKX, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, Brave Wallet, Trust Wallet, Phantom and others) appear by name instead of fighting over window.ethereum. Wallets not detected are shown as Not installed with their official download link. Only the wallet you tap will be asked to connect.
